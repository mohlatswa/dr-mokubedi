    About the practice

    Your local family doctor in Letlhabile and Brits

    Dr. LG Mokubedi Inc. was founded by Dr. Lehlogonolo George Mokubedi, a general medical practitioner committed to accessible, quality healthcare close to home.

    Our main practice in the Letlhabile Medical Centre is open seven days a week with extended weekday hours, so you can see a doctor before or after work. Our Brits branch at Brits Kerkmart serves patients from Monday to Saturday.

    Our services

    Comprehensive care under one roof

    From routine check-ups to diagnostics and minor procedures, we look after every stage of your family's health.

    General GP Consultations

    Diagnosis and treatment of everyday illnesses, infections, injuries and general health concerns for adults and children.

    Chronic Disease Management

    Ongoing care and medication for chronic conditions, with regular monitoring to keep you well.

    Mother & Baby Clinic

    Antenatal care for expecting mothers and dedicated clinic support for mom and baby.

    Women's Health

    Pap smears, sonar and family planning services in a private, respectful setting.

    Sonar & Ultrasound

    On-site ultrasound imaging, including pregnancy scans, without a separate referral trip.

    ECG & Spirometry

    Heart rhythm (ECG) and lung function (spirometry) testing to support accurate diagnosis.

    Wound Care

    Cleaning, dressing and follow-up care for cuts, burns and chronic wounds.

    Pensioner Care

    Attentive, patient-centred care for our senior patients and their ongoing health needs.

    Wellness, IV Drips & Circumcisions

    Wellness clinic check-ups, IV drip therapy and safe medical circumcisions.
